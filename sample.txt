📝 Summarize
Converts a lengthy document into a short and clear summary.
Identifies the main ideas and important information.
Helps users understand documents quickly.
Reduces the time required to read large documents.
Uses the AI model to generate the summary from the uploaded document.
⭐ Key Features
Document Upload – Users can upload a document for analysis.
Text Chunking – Large documents are divided into smaller sections.
Text Embedding – Sentence Transformers converts text into numerical embeddings.
Vector Storage – ChromaDB stores document embeddings for retrieval.
Semantic Search – Finds document sections that are most relevant to the user's question.
RAG – Retrieved information is provided to the language model as context for generating the answer. The original RAG approach combines retrieval with generation to improve knowledge-intensive question answering.
AI Question Answering – Users can ask questions about their uploaded document.
Chat History – Previous questions and answers can be displayed in the sidebar.
Personality Selection – Users can select different response styles such as Professor or Friendly.
